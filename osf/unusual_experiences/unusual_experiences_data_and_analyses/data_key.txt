PCS (Phenomenological Control Scale)
-----
PCSscore: mean of response to 10 PCS scale items:

 1handlowersubj: Hand lowering suggestion subjective response 
 2handtogethersubj: Hands together suggestion subjective response 
 3mosquitosubj: Mosquito suggestion subjective response 
 4TasteAverage: Mean subjective response to sweet and sour taste suggestions.
 5rigidsubj: Arm rigidity suggestion subjective response 
 6immobilizationsubj: Arm immobilisation suggestion subjective response 
 7musicsubj: Music suggestion subjective response 
 8ballssubj: Negative visual hallucination suggestion subjective response 
 9amnesiasubj: Amnesia suggestion subjective response 
 10spaceGeoMean: Mean subjective response to post-session suggestion urge and memory items.

10spacebarsubj1: post-session suggestion urge subjective response
10spacebarsubj2: post-session suggestion memory subjective response
4Tastesweetsubj: sweet taste suggestion subjective response
4Tastesoursubj: sour taste suggestion subjective response

-----------------------------

anomalous experience/belief
---------------------------
Q1_1 to Q11_1: Response to experience questionnaire items
Belief questions_1 to Belief questions_8: Response to  belief questionnaire items

past_life_experience: mean of Q1_1 to Q3_1
out_of_body_experience: mean of Q4_1 to Q6_1
spirit_world_experience: mean of Q7_1 to Q11_1

past_life_belief: mean of Belief questions_1 and Belief questions_2
out_of_body_belief: mean of Belief questions_3 and Belief questions_4
spirit_world_belief: mean of Belief questions_5 to Belief questions_8

Combined_anomalous_experience: mean of past_life_experience, out_of_body_experience and spirit_world_experience

------------------------------------
DES (Dissociative Experiences Scale)

Q25_1 to Q25_28: Response to 28 DES items

DES_score: Mean response to Q25_1 to Q25_28
DES_mean_centred: mean centred DES_score
PCS_meancentred: mean centred PCS_score
DESxPCS: product of DES_mean_centred and PCS_meancentred

----------------------------------
Flow scale

Flow questionnaire 1_1 to Flow questionnaire 1_10 response to flow scale vacuuming questions

Flow questionnaire 2_1 to Flow questionnaire 2_10 response to flow scale essay writing questions

Flow questionnaire 3_1 to Flow questionnaire 3_10 response to flow scale exercise questions

Flow_vacuuming: Mean of Flow questionnaire 1_1 to Flow questionnaire 1_10
Flow_essay_writing: Mean of Flow questionnaire 2_1 to Flow questionnaire 2_10
Flow_exercise: Flow questionnaire 3_1 to Flow questionnaire 3_10
Flow_combined: mean of Flow_vacuuming, Flow_essay_writing and Flow_exercise
