Rubber hand illusion
------------------------

Quartile_by_Subjective_SWASH: 1 = lowest 25% by hypnotisability scores, 4= highest 25% hypnotisability scores

PD = proprioceptive drift

S1-S4 = subjective report of agreement with statements.

PD_pre and post = reported position of hand (in cm) before and after induction

Instruction condition: 1 = synchronous instruction, 2 = asynchronous instruction, 3 = no instruction
